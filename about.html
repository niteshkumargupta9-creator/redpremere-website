Who We Are

Supply, Software,
One Firm.

Red Premere Enterprises is a sole proprietorship based in Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh, run by Nitesh Kumar Gupta. It does two things: it supplies stationery, printed material and uniforms to schools and institutions, and it builds websites and web applications.

Those look like different businesses. In practice they are the same one. A school that needs 400 notebooks in March usually also needs a website its parents can actually find, and a notice board that is not a photograph on WhatsApp. Working on both sides means we understand how an institution actually buys — the requirement list, the quotation, the purchase order, the invoice — and we work to that rhythm instead of asking you to work to ours.

Send a requirement list. Get a written quotation in 24 hours. Take delivery against a proper GST invoice.

The firm is GST registered and holds Udyam (MSME) registration. Our own consumer products — bottles, insulated drinkware and printed merchandise — are sold through our e-commerce branch at store.redpremere.com, which we also built ourselves.

Our Values

How We Operate

Quoted, not guessed

Every quotation is itemised against your actual requirement list. GST is shown separately. The number you approve is the number you are invoiced.

Written before spoken

Requirements, quotations and delivery schedules go in writing. It protects you at audit time and it protects us from misremembering.

Locally present

We are in Varanasi. We can deliver in person across the district, and we work in Hindi and English equally.

You own what we build

Your domain is registered in your name and the site is yours to keep. Renewal is a service, not a hostage arrangement.

Registered and invoiced

GST and Udyam registered. Proper tax invoice on every order, so B2B buyers can claim input tax credit and institutions can file cleanly.

Small on purpose

You deal with the person doing the work. No account manager relaying messages, no ticket queue.
